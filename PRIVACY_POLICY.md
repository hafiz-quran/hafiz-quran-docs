# Privacy Policy for Hafiz Quran

**Last Updated: July 14, 2026**

## Introduction

Hafiz Quran ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you use our Quran memorization application, and how.

## Summary

**In plain language:**
- You can use the app as a guest — no account required.
- If you choose to sign in, we collect your name and either your email (English) or phone number (Uzbek) to create your account and sync your progress.
- Guest data stays on your device only.
- Optional voice recordings you make to check your recitation are uploaded to our secure storage only while you are signed in.
- We use Sentry for crash reports and do not use any advertising or third-party marketing trackers.
- We do not sell your data to anyone.

## Information We Collect

### If You Use the App as a Guest

You can skip sign-in and use the app as a guest. In that case:
- Your profile (name, birth year, and optional gender), memorization progress, lesson completions, review schedules, and statistics are stored **locally on your device only** using an on-device database.
- None of this data is transmitted to our servers.

### If You Create an Account

Signing in is optional. If you choose to sign in, we collect the following so we can create your account and sync your progress across devices:
- **Account identifiers:** Email address and password, or Google account (email and display name), for English users; or phone number (verified by one-time code) for Uzbek users.
- **Profile information:** Your name, birth year, and optional gender.
- **Engagement summary:** A daily total of minutes practiced, used to support streaks and accountability features. We do not upload your detailed activity logs.

Authentication and account data are handled through [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), our backend provider.

### Voice Recordings (Optional)

The app includes an optional self-check feature that lets you record your recitation to listen back and check for mistakes. This requires microphone access, which you grant explicitly.
- If you are signed in, these recordings are uploaded to our secure storage (Supabase) so the feature can process them.
- If you are a guest, recordings are not uploaded.
- Recordings are only made when you actively use this feature.

### On-Device Usage Data

- The app keeps a short-term log of in-app activity (such as which screens you open) on your device to calculate the practice-time statistics it shows you. This log is retained for about 7 days and is not uploaded in raw form.

## Third-Party Services

### Crash Reporting (Sentry)

We use [Sentry](https://sentry.io/privacy/) for crash reporting to help us improve app stability and fix bugs. Sentry may collect device information (model, operating system version), app version, and crash logs, including a screenshot and view snapshot of the app at the moment of a crash. We configure Sentry to scrub authentication tokens and other secrets before sending, and we do not send additional personally identifying data by default.

### Backend and Authentication (Supabase)

We use Supabase to store account data, sync signed-in users' profiles and daily engagement summaries, and host optional voice recordings. See [Supabase's Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

### Quran Content and Audio

- Quran text and streak data are retrieved from the [Quran Foundation](https://quran.foundation/) API.
- Audio recitations are streamed or downloaded from public content providers (such as quran.com and quranicaudio.com).
- These requests fetch content only and do not send your personal information to those providers.

## How We Use Your Information

- To create and manage your account (if you sign in).
- To sync your memorization progress and settings across your devices.
- To provide streaks, review scheduling, and practice-time statistics.
- To provide the optional recitation self-check feature.
- To diagnose crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Data Security

- On-device data is stored using your device's built-in secure storage mechanisms.
- Authentication tokens are stored in your device's secure keychain/keystore.
- Data transmitted to our backend is sent over encrypted connections.

## Children's Privacy

Hafiz Quran is intended for learners of all ages, including children. For account features, we collect only the limited profile information described above. We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is necessary to provide the app's features. Parents can allow their children to use the app as a guest, in which case data stays on the device. If you believe a child has provided us information you would like removed, please contact us.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- Guest data remains on your device until you delete the app; deleting the app removes all local data permanently.
- For signed-in users, account and synced data are retained until you request deletion.
- To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the data associated with your account.
- Request deletion of your account and its data.
- Use the app entirely as a guest, keeping your data on your device.

## Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs:
- **Internet / Network access:** To sign in, sync (for signed-in users), and download Quran text and audio.
- **Microphone:** Only for the optional recitation self-check feature.

You can deny optional permissions, but the features that rely on them will not work.
